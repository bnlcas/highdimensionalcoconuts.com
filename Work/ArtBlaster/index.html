The NVIDO Museum of Art: one of every masterpiece, bought by founder Jetson Hang with one afternoon of stock gains.

You are a local billionaire (never mind which one). Jetson lent you the keys for the night. Rack up the biggest damage bill in history before the insurance adjusters arrive.

Every work here is real, with real prices from auctions and insurance records. Aim at a piece to see what it's worth, and read its placard to learn who made it and what has happened to it before. Smashing a Caravaggio is not the same as throwing soup at a Rothko.

You start with nothing but a tube of lipstick. The soup, crowbar and paintball marker are hidden around the museum; follow the beams of light. The sledgehammer and flamethrower sit in time-locked cases that only open once your bill is big enough.
