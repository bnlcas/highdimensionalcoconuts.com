StrobeCamera

A camera for playing with motion, time, light and color.

What does it do?

StrobeCamera layers time-delayed copies of you over the live camera feed, like a stroboscopic photograph that keeps moving. Each echo can be shifted around the color wheel, or split into red, green and blue channels taken at slightly different moments.

It is a small philosophical toy, inspired by Eadweard Muybridge's motion studies and Sergey Prokudin-Gorsky's three-exposure color photographs. Wave your arms and see what happens.

  • Choose 2–9 echoes, spaced 0.1–1 second apart
  • Hue mode: rainbow trails across the stack of echoes
  • Prokudin mode: separate red, green and blue in time
  • Save photos and videos to your Photo Library
