Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

StrobeCamera is made by Ben Lucas Heavy Industries. StrobeCamera does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

Camera

StrobeCamera uses your camera to create its effect. Camera frames are processed on your device, in memory, and are discarded within a few seconds. While you record a video, it is written to a temporary file on your device, which is deleted once the video is saved to your Photo Library. Nothing is ever uploaded or sent anywhere.

Photo Library

When you choose to save a photo or video, StrobeCamera asks for permission to add it to your Photo Library. The app cannot read or view any of the photos or videos already in your library.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

StrobeCamera has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the internet.

Children

Because StrobeCamera collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be filed as an issue on GitHub or sent to info@highdimensionalcoconuts.com.
