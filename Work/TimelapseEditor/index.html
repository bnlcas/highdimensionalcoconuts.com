⏱️ Chron Studio

An app dedicated to the inexorable passasge of time

This is a simple timelapse editor for iOS. I got into timelapse photography during the pandemic, and it is a wonderful thing to explore. This app contains the basic editing functions that I consistently used touch up a timelapse. I hope you find it useful too. Go out and photograph a tree, capture a sunset and observe the passage of time.


What Does this Do?

This app lets you upload either a video (timelapse or not), or a collection of photographs. You can then adjust the playback rate to make it play slower or faster.
It also has the functions to trim the ends of your timelapse, crop the edges and stabilize the footage, so you can export to your photo roll or share with friends.
